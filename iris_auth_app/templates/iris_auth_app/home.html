How it works

Three steps connect your identity to your campus access.

01 / ENROLL

Register Your Identity

Students enter their registration details and enroll an iris template with an image upload or camera capture.

02 / VERIFY

Verify Your Identity

The server compares the submitted iris image with active, compatible registered templates.

03 / ACCESS

Access Your Campus Portal

A successful unique match opens the dashboard permitted for that account type.
